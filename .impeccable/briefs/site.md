# Surface brief: whole site (home + recipe book + recipe pages + articles + about)

Scope: home = Persuade; recipe pages and articles = Read with an Operate cooking mode.
Audience/job: home cooks pick a dish, cook it from a phone, watch the video, subscribe.
Proof/content: real recipes, photos, videos and testimonials from smachnostudio.com.
Build path: code-led (no image generation available; launcher not run).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The owner's night-sky logo becomes speckled cobalt enamelware. The site is an enamel kitchen shelf: speckled cobalt fields carry the brand, video and voices; glossy white enamel carries the recipe book. Refuses the white food-blog grid with orange buttons, and the cream/serif/terracotta food default.

OWN-WORLD: cobalt enamel #1D3B9E speckled with white and logo-gold flecks; glossy enamel white #F4F6FB; rim ink #0E1A3F; logo gold #D9CC8E; tomato enamel #D8432B only for heat (play, timers). Prata display (echoes the logo's serif S), Onest for text. Every container is enamelware: rounded rim (ink outline + inner highlight), pill tags like enamel labels.

STORY: The dish glows on blue; the visitor opens it, checks ingredients, cooks along in cooking mode with timers, watches the video, subscribes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: full-bleed speckled cobalt whose specks twinkle as stars. Left: Prata headline of the recipe of the day at display scale, one-line description, time, two actions (Готовить по шагам / Смотреть видео). Right: the dish photo on a white enamel tray with ink rim and a gold "Рецепт дня" tag, overlapping into the white section below.

FORM: enamel kitchenware × the logo's night sky, #1 of the ordered list. Seed key: none (concept-seed unavailable). Signature interaction: cooking mode whose step timers ring a synthesized enamel-bowl chime.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
